# RentalShield NYC Demo Deck Outline

## Deck Purpose

This deck is designed as a controlled MVP demo presentation for RentalShield NYC. It positions the product as an AI-powered rental scam screening assistant for Manhattan apartment hunters, not as a guarantee that a listing is legitimate.

## Visual Direction

- Modern AI technology presentation.
- Deep navy, slate, warm off-white, muted green, amber, and red accents.
- Trust-oriented NYC rental vibe.
- Clean cards, architecture diagrams, score visuals, and concise product copy.
- Avoid fear-heavy scam imagery; emphasize clarity, confidence, and decision support.

## Slide List

1. Title: RentalShield NYC
2. The Problem: NYC rental decisions are fast, expensive, and high-risk.
3. Why Now: Scammers exploit speed, remote searches, and fragmented verification.
4. What It Does: Converts listing evidence into an explainable scam-risk estimate.
5. User Flow: Submit evidence, run checks, receive score and next steps.
6. Feature System: URL parsing, message analysis, screenshots, rent context, contact lookup.
7. Results Experience: Score gauge, summary, ranked factors, verified facts, next steps.
8. AI + Verification Pipeline: Bedrock plus deterministic provider-backed tools.
9. MCP-Compatible Architecture: Modular tool orchestration and traceable checks.
10. Tech Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Supabase, Bedrock, RentCast, Google, NY Open Data.
11. MVP Scope: Manhattan V1, no auth, informational risk estimate, no guarantee.
12. Demo Scenarios + Roadmap: Clean listing, verified contact, suspicious pressure, address mismatch, future roadmap.

## Core Demo Message

RentalShield NYC helps renters slow down before they send money, documents, or personal information. It reads the listing, message, screenshots, rent context, and contact identity signals, then explains what looks normal, what looks risky, and what still needs verification.

## Safe Product Claims

- Screens rental listings for scam risk.
- Uses AI to extract rental-specific signals from messages and screenshots.
- Checks rent context against available market data.
- Supports official NY broker/agent name lookup through active-license data.
- Provides explainable ranked risk factors.
- Shows what checks ran and what could not be verified.
- Built around an MCP-compatible modular tool architecture.

## Claims To Avoid

- Guarantees a listing is legitimate.
- Proves fraud.
- Fully verifies all brokers.
- Replaces legal advice.
- Works equally well outside Manhattan V1.
